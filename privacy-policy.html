In short

DiagOne (the "app") does not collect, sell or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Everything the app measures or saves stays on your phone.

What the app uses, and why

ItemWhyLeaves your phone?
CameraLive preview for the camera test, and the flashlight test.No. Nothing is recorded or saved.
MicrophoneLevel meter and waveform for the microphone test; a 4-second test recording is played back to you.No. The test recording is kept in memory only and discarded when you leave the page.
LocationShows coordinates, accuracy, speed and a small map position in the GPS test.No. It is not stored or sent.
Map tiles (optional, off until you switch it on)If you switch on the street map in the GPS test, the app downloads small map pictures from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org) for the area shown.Only the map-tile requests. That server can see your IP address and which map area you are viewing, which shows roughly where you are (an area of a few hundred metres). Your exact coordinates are not sent. With the map off, nothing is requested. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy at osmfoundation.org.
Phone benchmarkMeasures processor, memory, graphics and storage speed on your phone and shows a score.No. Scores are saved on your phone only (until you clear them).
Installed apps (apps you can open)Lets you pick games in Game Zone and launch them, and shows their icons.No. Only the apps you choose are remembered, on your phone.
Device information (model, Android version, screen, memory, storage, battery, sensors, cameras)Shown on the specs and test screens, and in the report you choose to share.No, unless you press Share and send the report yourself.
Wi-Fi and network stateNetwork check: connection type, signal strength, link speed.No.
Close background appsThe memory cleanup button asks Android to close other apps' background processes. Android may restart them.No.
InternetWhen you press a network test, the app requests a small public page from Google's connectivity server (www.gstatic.com) several times to measure delay and packet loss. No personal data is sent. As with any web request, that server can see your IP address.Only that request, only when you press the test.

What is stored on your phone

Test results marked PASS or FAIL, your list of games, graphics profiles, touch calibration and training results, and your settings (for example full screen on or off). This stays on your phone. You can remove it at any time in Android Settings > Apps > DiagOne > Storage > Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

Sharing

We do not share data with anyone. If you press Share on a report or a graphics profile, the text is handed to the app you choose (for example a messaging app). That is your decision, and we do not receive it.

Third-party code

The app is built with the open-source Capacitor framework. It includes no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking libraries.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Security

Network traffic uses HTTPS only. The app does not back up its data to the cloud.

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be published at this address and in the app.
